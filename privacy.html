Privacy & Data Policy

Effective 7 June 2026

iCoderAgent ("the app", "we") is a mobile-first AI coding assistant. We built it to be privacy-preserving by design: your code, your API keys, and your conversations stay on your device or go directly to the services you choose — they are not routed to us for storage.

In short

  • We do not sell your data or show ads.
  • We do not run third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • Your API keys, tokens and passwords are stored only in the iOS Keychain on your device.
  • Your prompts and code go directly to the AI provider you configure, under that provider's own privacy policy.

Information stored on your device

  • Credentials — AI provider API keys, your GitHub token, database passwords, the relay session token, and MCP server tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain. They never leave your device except to authenticate directly with the service they belong to.
  • Projects & files — code and files you create are stored in the app's sandbox on your device.
  • Conversation history & memory — recent terminal/agent history and any project "memory" notes are stored locally so sessions persist.
  • Usage logs — token counts and estimated cost per session are written to a local log on your device to power the in-app usage report. These are not sent to us.

Information you send to AI providers

When you use the agent, the prompts you type, any files you attach, and relevant project code are sent directly from your device to the AI provider you selected (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI, or Microsoft Azure / GitHub Models). This data is handled under that provider's privacy policy and terms. Choose a provider you trust, and avoid sending secrets in prompts.

Account & the relay (optional)

If you create an account or use "Remote (laptop)", we process the email address you sign up with (or the relay email Apple provides for Sign in with Apple) to authenticate you. Passwords are stored only as a salted hash; we cannot read them.

The relay's sole job is to route traffic between your phone and your own laptop so you don't need to open a tunnel or expose a port. Code and commands pass through the relay in transit (over TLS) but are not stored by it. You can use the app fully on-device without an account ("Continue without an account").

MCP servers & integrations you add

If you connect an MCP server or other integration, the data the agent exchanges with it goes to that server, under its operator's policy. You control which servers are enabled.

Security

Secrets live in the iOS Keychain. Network traffic uses encrypted HTTPS/TLS. No security measure is perfect, but we minimize what leaves your device.

Data retention & your choices

  • Remove a credential any time in Settings (it is deleted from the Keychain).
  • Sign out to clear your relay session from the device.
  • Delete a project or its files in the Files tab.
  • Deleting the app removes all locally stored data, including Keychain items for the app.
  • To delete a relay account, contact us at the address below.

Children

iCoderAgent is intended for developers and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region).

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.
